Our Story

Experience makes the difference.

Opening and running a restaurant takes more than having a good concept or a great menu. It requires hundreds of decisions about costs, people, equipment, pricing, service and daily execution.

At Proof, our perspective comes from actively owning and operating restaurants. We understand that small decisions can have a significant impact on profitability and that most independent operators don't have unlimited time or money.

Proof exists to help coffee shops, cafés and quick-service restaurants navigate these decisions with more confidence and less waste.

Our Philosophy

Spend smarter. Build better. Keep improving.

A strong restaurant starts with thoughtful decisions, efficient systems and a realistic understanding of its numbers.
